1. Who Can Use Visited

Visited is intended for users who are at least 16 years old.

The App is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

If we become aware that personal data of a child under 16 has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete that data.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only data necessary to provide and operate Visited and data that you voluntarily provide while using the App.

Account Data

When you create and use a Visited account, we may process:

  • your email address;
  • authentication and account identifiers;
  • profile information you choose to provide;
  • profile image or avatar, if provided.

Passwords are handled through the authentication service used by Visited. Visited does not store your password in readable form.

Content You Add to Visited

When using the App, you may create and store information about places you have visited, including:

  • place names and titles;
  • descriptions;
  • ratings;
  • dates of visits;
  • tags or categories;
  • photographs;
  • location names;
  • geographic coordinates associated with a place.

This information is provided voluntarily by you as part of the core functionality of Visited.

Location Data

Visited allows you to associate geographic locations with places stored in the App.

Depending on the functionality you use, location information may include:

  • latitude and longitude;
  • a location selected manually on a map;
  • a location name.

Visited does not use location information for advertising or user profiling.

Where access to your device's location is required, the App will request the appropriate operating-system permission.

You may refuse or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-related functionality may then be unavailable.

Technical and Security Data

Our infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as:

  • IP address;
  • device or network information;
  • authentication events;
  • server logs;
  • timestamps;
  • information required to detect errors, abuse or unauthorized access.

We do not use this information for advertising or marketing profiling.

3. How We Use Personal Data

We process personal data in order to:

  • create and maintain your Visited account;
  • authenticate you and secure access to your account;
  • provide the functionality of the App;
  • store and display places, photographs and other content you add;
  • provide location and map-related functionality;
  • maintain the security and integrity of the service;
  • investigate technical problems, abuse or unauthorized access;
  • respond to privacy, support and account-deletion requests;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

We do not use personal data for targeted advertising.

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area ("EEA"), we process your personal data under the legal bases provided by the GDPR.

Performance of a Contract – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR

Most processing necessary to provide Visited is based on the performance of a contract with you or taking steps at your request before entering into such a contract.

This includes processing necessary to:

  • create and operate your account;
  • authenticate you;
  • store your places and associated content;
  • store photographs;
  • provide core App functionality.

Legitimate Interests – Article 6(1)(f) GDPR

We may process limited technical and security information where necessary for our legitimate interests in:

  • securing the App and its infrastructure;
  • preventing fraud and abuse;
  • diagnosing technical problems;
  • maintaining service reliability.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider whether those interests are overridden by your rights and freedoms.

Legal Obligations – Article 6(1)(c) GDPR

We may process or retain information where required to comply with applicable law or a legally binding request.

Consent – Article 6(1)(a) GDPR

Where a specific feature requires consent under applicable law, we will request it separately.

Device permissions, such as access to your location or photographs, are managed through the permission mechanisms provided by your operating system.

You can revoke device permissions through your device settings.

5. Service Providers

Supabase

Visited uses Supabase for backend services, including:

  • user authentication;
  • database services;
  • storage of user-generated content and photographs;
  • related backend infrastructure.

The primary Supabase infrastructure used by Visited is hosted in the European Union, in the AWS Europe (Ireland) region (eu-west-1).

Supabase processes personal data on our behalf as a service provider/data processor, subject to its applicable contractual and data-protection obligations.

MapLibre

Visited uses MapLibre technology to display interactive maps.

MapLibre is an open-source mapping library. The library itself does not constitute an advertising or analytics service.

Map content may be obtained from external map or geocoding providers. If such providers process personal data as part of providing their services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant providers and processing where required.

6. International Data Transfers

The primary backend infrastructure used by Visited is hosted within the European Union.

However, some service providers or their subprocessors may process limited personal data outside the European Economic Area ("EEA"), for example when providing technical, security or support services.

Where personal data protected by the GDPR is transferred outside the EEA, appropriate safeguards will be used as required by applicable data-protection law.

Depending on the destination and provider, these safeguards may include an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Account information and content associated with your account are generally retained for as long as your Visited account remains active.

When you delete your account or request account deletion, personal data associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period, unless retention of particular information is required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims.

Technical logs and backup copies may remain for a limited period as part of normal security, backup and disaster-recovery processes before being automatically deleted or overwritten.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Visited account and associated personal data.

Where account deletion is available directly in the App, you may use that functionality.

You may also initiate an account-deletion request outside the App by following the instructions provided on the Visited account-deletion webpage.

If deletion is requested by email, we may require reasonable verification that the request originates from the owner of the account.

For example, we may ask you to send the request from the email address associated with your Visited account.

After verification, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with the account, subject to any limited retention required by applicable law.
