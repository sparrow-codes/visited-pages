Account deletion request

You can request deletion of your Visited account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

Delete Your Account in the App

If you still have access to the Visited app, you can use the account deletion option available in your profile or account settings.

This is the recommended method because it allows the request to be initiated directly from your authenticated account.

Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the App, you can request account deletion by email.

Send your request to:
sparrow-codes@protonmail.com

Please send the request from the same email address that is associated with your Visited account.

You may use the following subject: Delete my Visited account.

Verification

To protect users against unauthorized deletion requests, we may need to verify that the request was made by the owner of the account.

Sending the request from the email address associated with your Visited account will normally be used as part of this verification process.

If additional verification is reasonably required, we may contact you before deleting the account.

What Will Be Deleted

Once your request has been verified, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, including, where applicable:

  • your Visited user account;
  • your email address and profile information;
  • your profile image or avatar;
  • places you created;
  • descriptions and ratings;
  • visit dates;
  • tags or categories associated with your places;
  • location information and geographic coordinates;
  • photographs stored as part of your Visited content.

Data That May Be Retained

Some limited information may be retained where required by applicable law or where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal claims, backup, or disaster-recovery purposes.

Backup copies may remain temporarily until they are automatically deleted or overwritten as part of normal backup-retention processes.

Processing Time

Account-deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after verification.

Requests concerning personal data are handled in accordance with applicable data-protection law, including the GDPR where applicable.
